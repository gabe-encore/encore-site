Hosting clients at a game: common questions

How much does a private suite cost for a corporate event?

A single suite for 18–30 guests typically runs $15K–$35K depending on the game. Large lounges for 40–50 guests start around $15K plus food and drinks.

Can we host an event on a non-game day?

Yes. On dark nights, arenas can host private receptions and even on-court events, subject to the venue's calendar.

How far ahead should we book?

Ideally six to eight weeks. Many venues need 45–60 days for non-game events, and the best game nights sell out early.

What's included, and what costs extra?

You pay for the space and food and drinks. Experiences like tours, court time and a leadership welcome are often included, and every package spells out exactly what each one involves.

How does internal approval work?

You get an approval-ready page with the itemized pricing and a timed run-of-show, and your space is held for 24 hours while you get sign-off.

Who do we deal with at the venue?

Encore's AI Coordination Agent handles the back-and-forth across the venue's teams, and a dedicated host from the venue's premium team looks after your group on the night.
