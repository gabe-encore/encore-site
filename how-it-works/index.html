How It Works

From "they're in town" to "it's booked," in minutes.

Encore finds corporate groups coming to your market, gives them a turnkey event to say yes to, and gets every team at your venue to sign off at once. Your premium team closes.

1

AI Radar finds the demand

Tracks every conference, summit and offsite coming to your market, identifies the companies with budget and the people who buy hospitality, and matches them to your open dates.

2

The buyer picks a package

Through an Encore outreach link or your venue planning page, buyers choose a turnkey package or make their own within budget, with transparent pricing and a 24-hour hold.

3

The AI Coordination Agent gets every green light

Events, F&B, ops and merch sign off in parallel, not one after another. The buyer gets an approval-ready plan to forward internally.

4

Your team closes

The deal lands with your premium rep, fully scoped and approved on both sides. Every booking is attributed from first touch to closed revenue.

One booking. Full visibility on both sides.

Your team and the buyer work from the same live status, so nobody waits on an email to find out where things stand.
