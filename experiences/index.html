Experiences

A sense of what's possible.

These are illustrative — a feel for the kind of night Encore builds, not a fixed package or a real event. Every one starts the same way: you tell us who you're trying to impress, and we build around that.

Every scenario below is an illustrative example, not a real client event.

Field Marketing & Demand Gen

The Prospect Suite

Illustrative

15–25 top-target accounts in a private suite. Pregame reception, a tip-off dinner timed to the game, and enough room for your AEs to work without it feeling like work.

Executive Relations

The Executive Box

Illustrative

8–12 senior buyers and your leadership team in a small private box. Curated food and beverage, structured time, and no pitch deck in sight.

Channel & Alliances

The Partner Night

Illustrative

30–40 partners and reps in a semi-private club space. Game-night energy, easy for reps to bring a plus-one, built to feel like a reward, not a meeting.

Late-Stage Deals

The Closer

Illustrative

A handful of guests, a top-tier suite, postgame access. Built for the conversation that happens between periods, not across a table.

Instead Of Another Dinner

The Conference Alternative

Illustrative

Off-site, private, and timed around the game — so nobody's rushing back for a keynote, and your invite is the one people actually open.

None of these are a menu. Tell us who you're trying to impress.
