About Encore

Companies come to town with budget. They should leave with a night at your arena.

Why Encore exists

The demand is already here. It just goes to the steakhouse.

Every week, companies fly into cities for conferences with money set aside to entertain clients and prospects. Most of it ends up at a restaurant, while suites and dark nights at the arena down the street go unsold.

The problem isn't interest. Buyers love the idea of hosting at a game. It's that nobody connects the two: venues can't see who's coming, and buyers can't see what's possible or get it approved fast enough.

Encore closes that gap. AI Radar finds the demand, turnkey packages make it easy to say yes, and the AI Coordination Agent gets every team at the venue aligned in minutes. The venue's premium team closes the deal.
